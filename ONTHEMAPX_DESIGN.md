# ONTHEMAPX Design System

## 1. Brand idea

ONTHEMAPX marks the place where a person's taste and heart remain. The diamond is the starting point, the hand-drawn route is the process of discovery, and X is the destination where the treasure lies.

**Core positioning:** 발견 · 취향 · 마음

## 2. Palette

- Ink Black `#0B0B0D`: attitude, contrast, premium dark canvas
- Signature Magenta `#D80068`: primary brand accent and discovery energy
- Route Red `#E5352B`: hand-drawn paths and directional marks only
- Silver: metallic gradient from `#70767A` through `#F7F7F3` to `#9CA2A5`
- Warm Ivory `#F1EBE3`: handmade warmth and editorial paper surface
- Paper `#FBF8F4`: default light canvas

Use magenta for primary CTA and major brand moments. Use route red sparingly for dotted paths, annotations, and X marks. Do not use both as equal competing button colors in the same local composition.

## 3. Typography

- Display: Iowan Old Style / Baskerville / Times New Roman, large and editorial
- Text/UI: system sans-serif / Noto Sans KR fallback
- Display hierarchy favors 48–118px, weight 400, tight tracking
- Body: 15–18px, line-height 1.6
- Eyebrow: 12px uppercase, 0.18em tracking

## 4. Shapes

- Product imagery: tall ovals, soft arches, and restrained 18–28px corners
- Editorial panels: 28–42px radius
- Controls: 999px pill only for actions and filters
- Route path: 3px stroke, dotted/dashed, rounded caps

## 5. Image direction

- Mix close product detail with worn/lifestyle imagery
- Prefer warm paper, books, wood, denim, and neutral clothing
- Let silver and beads catch natural or soft directional light
- Avoid sterile marketplace grids on the brand main page
- Preserve irregularity and handmade character without looking unfinished

## 6. Page structure

1. Main: emotional brand introduction and selected routes
2. Brand Story: positioning, symbols, color and logo system
3. Collection: filterable product/lookbook archive
4. Reference: domestic handmade accessory benchmark research
5. Marketing: Instagram-first execution plan and KPIs

## 7. Voice

Short, intimate, and direct. Describe products as personal coordinates, routes, markers, and treasures. Avoid excessive luxury clichés. The brand is emotional but not sentimental, handmade but not rustic, and distinctive without appearing loud.

## 8. Do / Don't

### Do
- Repeat Diamond → Route → X across touchpoints
- Use the silver wordmark on black or magenta canvases
- Give every collection item a route/point/treasure naming logic
- Keep product information simple and legible

### Don't
- Turn the full site into a Pinterest clone
- Use generic blue commerce CTAs
- Mix too many accent colors
- Treat the X only as decoration; it must signal a destination or decision
